💡 WATTWISE INSIGHTS

Smart Energy Insights

Simple tips to reduce electricity consumption, lower your bill and use energy efficiently.

❄️

Use AC Efficiently

Set your air conditioner around 24–26°C and keep doors and windows closed while it is running.

💡

Switch Off Lights

Turn off lights when leaving a room and prefer energy-efficient LED bulbs.

🔌

Reduce Standby Power

Unplug chargers and appliances when they are not being used to avoid unnecessary electricity usage.

🧊

Maintain Your Refrigerator

Avoid keeping the refrigerator door open for long periods and maintain proper temperature settings.

🌀

Use Fans Wisely

Switch off fans when leaving the room and clean them regularly for better performance.

📊

Monitor Your Usage

Use WattWise Analyzer regularly to understand which appliances consume the most energy.

🌱 Small Changes, Big Savings

Reducing unnecessary electricity usage can lower your monthly electricity bill and help conserve energy.
